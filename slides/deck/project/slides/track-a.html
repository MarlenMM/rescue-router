Track A results. The curve shows mIoU against the number of labelled photos, with 95 percent bootstrap intervals. From one to five photos the gain is real, plus .14. Between five and ten I could not measure any gain, and from 20 to 50 it jumps again by .10. On the full test set the 50-shot model reaches .472. The dashed line is PSPNet, trained on all 1,445 labelled photos: .848. Most of the gap is flooded roads: .29 against .82 — shallow brown water and a dry road look very similar from above. And transfer: the FloodNet model applied to a different hurricane with zero new labels gets .32 on roads, buildings and water; with just 20 labels from the new disaster it gets .63. So every new disaster needs a few of its own labels — which is exactly the few-shot setting.
