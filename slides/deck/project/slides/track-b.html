Track B is the system around the map. The router finds the cheapest way from the entry point to every house: truck on clear roads, then a boat across water, then on foot — with vehicle widths, parked cars and tree canopy taken into account. Left is the predicted map, middle the same street routed on the human labels — the same house needs a boat on both. Right, the hurricane-Michael canal: truck to the canal, boat across, walk to the house. Against the labelled map the router finds the houses that are cut off by road with F1 .759 on the full test.
