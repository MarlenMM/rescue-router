    About

    A drone photo is mapped into five classes by Chameleon, adapted from only 50 labelled photos (FloodNet) or 20 (RescueNet). A router on 4×4-pixel cells then finds the cheapest way from the image edge to each house: truck on dry ground (roads, or yards at 4× cost; never where water is likely), then a boat across water. Same router on the true map for comparison.

    FloodNet 50-shot: mIoU .47 on the full 448-image test (PSPNet with 1,445 labels: .85 on these classes). Two scenes are honest failures: shallow water read as dry land.
