    About

    A drone photo is mapped into five classes by Chameleon, adapted from only 50 labelled photos (FloodNet) or 20 (RescueNet); 30 more labelled validation photos per dataset chose the checkpoint, the decode thresholds and the router settings. A router on 4×4-pixel cells then finds the cheapest way from the entry point to each house: truck on dry ground (roads, or yards at 4× cost; never where water is likely), then a boat across water, then the last stretch on foot (never back into a vehicle). Vehicles have a width: a truck needs about 2.5 m between buildings and a boat about 1.6 m, so neither squeezes through the gap between two houses; people on foot can. The labelled map is the same router run on the human labels: the datasets label pixels, not reachability.

    Confidence: the prediction is routed twice, with the normal off-road rule (land where p_wet < 0.3 for FloodNet, 0.2 for RescueNet, each tuned on the dataset's validation photos) and a cautious one (p_wet < 0.05 / 0.15). Houses a truck reaches even on the cautious map are confident (100 % truly truck-reachable on FloodNet test photos); the others are marked "check first" (79 %). Overall about one in nine houses shown as truck-reachable was not (11 % on the full FloodNet test), mostly where the route crosses water the model missed. Minutes and metres are rough estimates (truck 20 km/h on roads, 5 off-road, boat 6, on foot 3; scale from the cars in the photo).

    FloodNet 50-shot: mIoU .51 on the full 448-image test (PSPNet with 1,445 labels: .85 on these classes). One showcase scene is an honest failure: shallow water read as dry land. A standard segmenter (DeepLabV3-ResNet50) fine-tuned on the same 50 photos, without the 30 tuning photos, does better: mIoU .58, and 8 % instead of 11 % of its truck-reachable houses are not. The Model switch shows it on the same 200 photos, with the same router and gates and its own statuses, tiers and per-photo IoU (its confident tier: 97 % truly truck-reachable on FloodNet, Chameleon's 100 %); Chameleon is the model as submitted. Houses the other model routes differently are marked in the house list and counted under the map; the sort "models disagree most" lists those photos first.

    Besides the 8 showcase scenes you can browse all 200 test photos of the evaluation split (100 FloodNet, 100 RescueNet), not picked by us: an orange count is the houses a truck cannot reach on the prediction, a pink dot marks a photo where prediction and labelled map disagree. "Water probability" shows what the model believes cell by cell. The address bar keeps the scene, house, entry point and view, so a link opens exactly this view.
